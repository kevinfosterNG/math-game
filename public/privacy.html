Math Quest privacy

Last updated September 26, 2026

Math Quest is a multiplication practice game. You can play without signing in, or use Google sign-in to save your own scores across devices.

Information we use

When you sign in, Google gives our authentication provider, Supabase, your basic account identity (such as your account ID, name, email address, and profile picture). We use it to recognize your account and keep your scores separate. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or school files.

For signed-in players, we store completed rounds, answers, correctness, difficulty, and response times in Supabase. Your scores are private to your account. Guest scores stay in this browser's memory for the current visit and are not saved to an account.

Who handles the information

Google handles sign-in. Supabase handles authentication and stores account-linked game progress. Our existing site host serves the game. We do not sell player information or use it for advertising.

Choices and deletion

You may play as a guest without a Google account. You can sign out at any time. To request deletion of your Math Quest account data or ask a privacy question, email math@fostes.org. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner or a parent or guardian.

Children

Parents and teachers should decide whether a child's school Google account may be used. Math Quest does not require a student to publish a name or scores to classmates.
